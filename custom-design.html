✦ Before we begin

Your questions.
Thoughtfully answered.

A few helpful starting points for your custom stationery journey.

Talk to our studio ↗
Can I begin without a clear design idea?

Yes. Share the mood of your celebration, your venue and anything you are drawn to. These can help guide the design conversation.

What is included in a custom design?

The scope is agreed with the studio before work begins. Your quote should set out the pieces, design work, revisions and production arrangements included.

Can my whole suite be coordinated?

Discuss the stationery you need, from save-the-dates to thank-you notes, so the chosen visual language can carry across the full suite.

How long does the process take?

Timing depends on the design scope, revision rounds and production finishes. Confirm a schedule with the studio around your celebration date.

When do I approve the final artwork?

Review names, dates, wording, quantities and layout before giving final approval. Production arrangements are confirmed after approval.
